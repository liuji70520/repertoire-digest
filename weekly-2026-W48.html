动态的 TCRβ 组库重构与小鼠及人肺移植物的功能障碍相关

Dynamic T-cell receptor repertoire remodeling is associated with murine and human lung allograft dysfunction

bioRxiv (immunology)/2026-11-23/预印本 Preprint
做了什么What

在小鼠原位肺移植模型和 23 例肺移植受者术后第一年的 90 份支气管肺泡灌洗细胞沉淀（BAL-cp）纵向样本上做 TCRβ 测序。小鼠同种异体移植物持续存在组织学损伤，组库重构明显强于同基因移植：第 28 与 56 天 productive clonality 更高（Cliff's δ=0.93，p=0.0087）、最大克隆频率更高（δ=0.93，p=0.014），两个时间点均完全秩分离；Morisita 组库重叠更大（p<0.0001），并富集反复出现的高频共享克隆型。人 BAL 样本中，事后发展为 CLAD 的患者在第 6 个月访视时 productive clonality 更高（p=0.03）。

为什么重要Why

活检只能看到某个时刻的一块组织，而“移植物的免疫重构”本来就是动态、空间异质的，这条用组库把它换成可重复采样的读数。对我们最直接的两点：一是临床读数的载体可以不是组织——灌洗液这类液基样本的克隆性同样能做纵向监测；二是 Morisita 重叠加共享克隆型这套指标，可以直接搬到我们自己的多时间点多组库比较里，比单看克隆数更有区分力。

TCRβ sequencing was performed on graft-infiltrating T cells from a murine orthotopic lung transplant model and on 90 longitudinal bronchoalveolar lavage cell-pellet samples from 23 lung transplant recipients during the first post-transplant year. Murine allografts showed persistent histologic injury and stronger repertoire remodelling than isografts: higher productive clonality and maximum productive frequency at days 28 and 56 (Cliff's δ=0.93; p=0.0087 and p=0.014, with complete rank separation), greater pairwise Morisita overlap (p<0.0001) and enrichment of recurrent high-frequency shared clonotypes; in human BAL, productive clonality was higher at the month-6 visit in patients who later developed CLAD (p=0.03).

Biopsy samples one moment and one piece of tissue, whereas alloimmune remodelling is dynamic and spatially heterogeneous; repertoire features turn it into a repeatable readout, and the Morisita-overlap plus shared-clonotype metrics transfer directly to our own multi-timepoint repertoire comparisons.

靶向天然与 Asp7 异构化 β-淀粉样蛋白的抗体候选：BCR 组库分析与克隆

BCR Repertoire Analysis and Cloning of Antibody Candidates Targeting Native and Asp7-Isomerized β-Amyloid

Immunology/2026-11-22/期刊 JournalIF 5.0JCR Q1中科院 3区
做了什么What

先用抗原四聚体分选免疫小鼠的 B 细胞，再做低起始量 bulk BCR-seq，得到可用克隆组库；通过克隆聚类优先挑出可能靶向 β-淀粉样蛋白及其 isoAsp7 修饰型（isoD7-Aβ1-16，与阿尔茨海默病相关）的 IGH 与 IGK 克隆型。观察到 IGHV8-12 与 IGKV1-117 反复使用，符合小鼠抗 Aβ 的经典应答；随后选 14 组 IGH-IGK 组合做重组表达，多株对 isoD7-Aβ 有可检测结合，其中一株偏好识别 isoD7-Aβ。作者指出重轻链配对仍然困难，链组合只是基于跨样本共现的推测，最终必须靠实验确证。

为什么重要Why

这条把“组库”当成抗体发现的入口，而不是描述性产物，和我们做克隆分析的落点一致；更值得记住的是作者自己点出的瓶颈——跨样本共现只能提出重轻链配对假设，不能确证。我们做配对组库时若想从克隆型直接指认功能抗体，同样需要额外的表达与结合验证这一层，不能只靠共现推断。

Antigen-tetramer sorting of B cells from immunized mice followed by low-input bulk BCR-seq produced informative clonal repertoires; clustering prioritised IGH and IGK clonotypes potentially targeting β-amyloid and its isoAsp7 proteoform (isoD7-Aβ1-16), with recurrent IGHV8-12 and IGKV1-117 usage consistent with canonical mouse anti-Aβ responses. Fourteen IGH-IGK combinations were expressed recombinantly; several bound isoD7-Aβ detectably, including one with preferential recognition, while the authors stress that heavy-light chain pairing remains difficult and that combinations inferred from cross-sample co-representation require experimental confirmation.

It treats the repertoire as an entry point for antibody discovery rather than a descriptive output, and names the real bottleneck: co-representation only proposes heavy-light pairings and must be confirmed experimentally, a caveat that applies equally to our own paired-repertoire work.

配对 αβ TCR 组库的结构聚类：超越序列相似性找回抗原特异性趋同

Structural clustering of paired αβ T-cell receptor repertoires recovers antigen-specific convergence beyond sequence similarity

bioRxiv (bioinformatics)/2026-11-24/预印本 Preprint
做了什么What

SPACE-TCR：把预测出的配对 αβ TCR 主链模型按 CDR 长度分层，在保守框架锚点上对齐，再按 CDR 区的 Cα RMSD 聚类。在独立整理的基准集（4,779 条配对 CD8 TCR、367 个表位）上，0.75 Å 阈值下 94.6% 的簇纯度 ≥0.9，与 ClusTCR 的 97.3% 接近，但聚到的唯一 CDR3β 数量是 ClusTCR 的两倍多（10.4% vs 4.7%），而其它“聚得更多”的方法纯度都明显更低；多特异性最低（5.4%）。22.7% 的高纯簇属于序列多样或序列无关，这类趋同家族基本被 CDR3β 相似性方法漏掉。背景噪声升高时优势更明显（89.2% 未注释噪声下纯度 30.4%，TCRMatch 为 15.0%）。

为什么重要Why

我们定义克隆时其实默认“序列相似就是同一克隆”，这对趋同演化与交叉反应是系统性偏差——不同序列完全可以折叠出相似的 CDR 环。这条给了一个可复核的替代口径，并诚实地量化了代价：纯度略降、覆盖面翻倍。对我们最实际的用处是，以后要给“跨个体共享克隆型”或“同一抗原的趋同应答”下更硬的结论时，结构聚类能捞到序列方法结构性漏掉的那部分，而且它在噪声背景下的稳健性更接近真实数据。

SPACE-TCR stratifies predicted paired αβ TCR backbone models by CDR length, aligns them on conserved framework anchors and clusters by Cα RMSD across the CDRs. On an independent benchmark of 4,779 paired CD8 TCRs spanning 367 epitopes, 94.6% of clusters at the 0.75 Å threshold reached purity ≥0.9 (versus 97.3% for ClusTCR) while clustering more than twice as many unique CDR3β (10.4% versus 4.7%), with methods that clustered more doing so at markedly lower purity; polyspecificity was the lowest (5.4%), 22.7% of high-purity clusters were sequence-diverse or sequence-unrelated families rarely recovered by CDR3β-similarity methods, and it retained higher purity as unannotated background rose (30.4% at 89.2% noise versus 15.0% for TCRMatch).

Clonal definition implicitly equates sequence similarity with clonal identity, a systematic bias for convergent evolution and cross-reactivity, since different sequences can adopt similar CDR loop geometry; this offers a testable alternative and honestly quantifies the trade-off, which matters when we make claims about shared clones or convergent responses.

多发性骨髓瘤 IGH/IGK 二代测序检测：CD138 阳性分选与未分选骨髓样本的比较

IGH and IGK Next-Generation Sequencing Testing in Multiple Myeloma: A Comparison of CD138-Positive Sorted and Unsorted Bone Marrow Samples

Annals of laboratory medicine/2026-11-25/期刊 JournalIF 3.9JCR Q1中科院 2区
做了什么What

比较 CD138 阳性浆细胞富集对 IGH/IGK 克隆型检出的影响：配对分样队列 N=20，另有分选常规化前后的非配对队列 N=60。配对队列里富集把 IGH 检出率从 65.0% 提到 85.0%、IGK 从 65.0% 提到 80.0%（数值上升，配对差异未达显著）；两等份都阳性的 15 例患者至少共享一个序列完全一致的克隆型；主导匹配克隆型的克隆 reads 占比 IGH 由中位 27.8% 升到 85.2%（P=0.0105），IGK 无显著变化（33.6%→36.9%，P=0.0942）。非配对队列检出差异 P=0.021（作者标注探索性）。结论写得克制：富集可能提高检出，但常规落地前需前瞻性配对验证。

为什么重要Why

这是 MRD 流水线的第一环——诊断时必须先抓到患者特异的 Ig 克隆型，抓不到后面全是空转。这篇文章给的实操口径很具体：低肿瘤负荷下富集能把 IGH 检出拉到 85% 量级，并把主导克隆的 reads 占比从 27.8% 显著推到 85.2%；但 IGK 没跟着改善，说明轻链侧的失败不全由细胞量决定（引物覆盖、体细胞高突变都会掺进来），这一步不能只靠“多上细胞”解决。

CD138-positive plasma cell enrichment was evaluated for IGH/IGK NGS clonotype detection in a paired split-sample cohort (N=20) and an unpaired cohort (N=60) before and after routine enrichment: in the paired cohort IGH detection rose from 65.0% to 85.0% and IGK from 65.0% to 80.0% (numeric only, not significant), all 15 patients positive in both aliquots shared at least one exact sequence-matched clonotype, and the clonal read fraction of dominant matched clonotypes increased for IGH (median 27.8% to 85.2%, P=0.0105) but not IGK (33.6% to 36.9%, P=0.0942); the unpaired cohort difference was P=0.021 (exploratory), and the authors call for prospective paired validation.

Clonotype identification at diagnosis gates the whole MRD pipeline; enrichment helps heavy-chain detection and dominant-clone read fraction but not kappa, showing light-chain failures are not purely a cell-quantity problem.

TCR 组库刻画免疫健康连续谱，并在亚健康状态中揭示提前的免疫衰老

TCR Repertoire Profiling Defines a Continuum of Immune Health and Reveals Premature Immunosenescence in Subclinical States

Research Square/2026-11-25/预印本 Preprint
做了什么What

用一个多任务深度学习框架同时做两件事：把免疫状态分成健康对照（HC）/亚健康（SHG）/过敏性鼻炎（AR）三类，以及预测“免疫年龄”。内部独立测试集上三分类准确率 90.28%、macro-F1 0.8977；健康人免疫年龄回归 R²=0.8244、MAE 3.39 岁。外部验证队列（新招 20 例 HC + 18 例 AR）里锁定模型正确分类 16/20 HC、12/18 AR，总体 73.68%（28/38），错判样本大多落到 SHG；外部 HC 免疫年龄 MAE 5.15 岁。亚健康与 AR 组的免疫年龄相对实际年龄平均加速 4 年以上。特征重要性上，健康状态分类主要由 Gini 系数（克隆集中度）和 V–J 配对偏倚驱动，CDR3 长度对两个任务都有贡献。

为什么重要Why

值得看的是它诚实的那一半：内部 90%、外部 74%，错判集中在亚健康——说明“亚健康”这一档在真实队列里是个吸收池，边界最不稳，任何拿它下结论的说法都要留余量。对我们更实际的是特征重要性：撑起分类的不是 CDR3 序列本身，而是克隆集中度和 V–J 配对偏倚，这两类指标算起来便宜、也不需要先解决克隆定义问题。换句话说，做组库健康度评估时先算清集中度与 V-J 偏倚，可能比继续堆克隆型注释更划算。预印本，未经同行评议。

A multi-task deep learning framework over TCR repertoires jointly classifies immune states (healthy control, subhealth, allergic rhinitis) and predicts immunological age: 90.28% accuracy and macro-F1 0.8977 internally, immune-age R²=0.8244 with MAE 3.39 years; on external validation (20 HC, 18 AR) the locked model classified 16/20 HC and 12/18 AR (73.68% overall, 28/38) with errors mostly assigned to subhealth, external immune-age MAE 5.15 years, and both subhealth and AR showed immune-age acceleration above 4 years; Gini coefficient and V–J pairing bias dominated state classification while CDR3 length contributed to both tasks.

The honest half matters most, with 90% internal dropping to 74% external and errors pooling in subhealth, while the feature importance shows cheap clonality metrics (Gini, V–J bias) carry the signal rather than CDR3 sequence per se.
