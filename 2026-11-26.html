多发性骨髓瘤 IGH/IGK 二代测序检测：CD138 阳性分选与未分选骨髓样本的比较

IGH and IGK Next-Generation Sequencing Testing in Multiple Myeloma: A Comparison of CD138-Positive Sorted and Unsorted Bone Marrow Samples

Annals of laboratory medicine/2026-11-25/期刊 JournalIF 3.9JCR Q1中科院 2区
做了什么What

比较 CD138 阳性浆细胞富集对 IGH/IGK 克隆型检出的影响：配对分样队列 N=20，另有分选常规化前后的非配对队列 N=60。配对队列里富集把 IGH 检出率从 65.0% 提到 85.0%、IGK 从 65.0% 提到 80.0%（数值上升，配对差异未达显著）；两等份都阳性的 15 例患者至少共享一个序列完全一致的克隆型；主导匹配克隆型的克隆 reads 占比 IGH 由中位 27.8% 升到 85.2%（P=0.0105），IGK 无显著变化（33.6%→36.9%，P=0.0942）。非配对队列检出差异 P=0.021（作者标注探索性）。结论写得克制：富集可能提高检出，但常规落地前需前瞻性配对验证。

为什么重要Why

这是 MRD 流水线的第一环——诊断时必须先抓到患者特异的 Ig 克隆型，抓不到后面全是空转。这篇文章给的实操口径很具体：低肿瘤负荷下富集能把 IGH 检出拉到 85% 量级，并把主导克隆的 reads 占比从 27.8% 显著推到 85.2%；但 IGK 没跟着改善，说明轻链侧的失败不全由细胞量决定（引物覆盖、体细胞高突变都会掺进来），这一步不能只靠“多上细胞”解决。

CD138-positive plasma cell enrichment was evaluated for IGH/IGK NGS clonotype detection in a paired split-sample cohort (N=20) and an unpaired cohort (N=60) before and after routine enrichment: in the paired cohort IGH detection rose from 65.0% to 85.0% and IGK from 65.0% to 80.0% (numeric only, not significant), all 15 patients positive in both aliquots shared at least one exact sequence-matched clonotype, and the clonal read fraction of dominant matched clonotypes increased for IGH (median 27.8% to 85.2%, P=0.0105) but not IGK (33.6% to 36.9%, P=0.0942); the unpaired cohort difference was P=0.021 (exploratory), and the authors call for prospective paired validation.

Clonotype identification at diagnosis gates the whole MRD pipeline; enrichment helps heavy-chain detection and dominant-clone read fraction but not kappa, showing light-chain failures are not purely a cell-quantity problem.

Peste des Petits Ruminants virus (PPRV) causes a highly contagious and often fatal disease in small ruminants, leading to substantial economic losses. The hemagglutinin-neuraminidase (HN) glycoprotein, a critical surface antigen of PPRV, is the primary target for neutralizing antibodies. In a previous study, we characterized a novel monoclonal antibody (mAb), designated HN 1D4-4D9 , which neutralizes PPRV by blocking viral adsorption and interfering with receptor interactions. This study aimed to identify the complementarity-determining regions (CDRs) in the mAb HN 1D4-4D9 that are essential for its neutralizing activity. The variable heavy (V H ) and variable light (V L ) chain sequences of HN 1D4-4D9 were determined for the first time using HybSeq HT next-generation sequencing. The most abundant V H and V L sequences were synthesized and recombinantly expressed. Western blot analysis showed that deletion of any one of CDR1, CDR2, or CDR3 in either the heavy or light chain completely abolished antigen-antibody binding, indicating that the sequence integrity of all six CDRs is required for recognition of the antigen. Crucially, functional evaluation demonstrated that the synthetically recombinant HN 1D4-4D9 mAb retained potent neutralizing activity against early PPRV infection in vitro. Identifying a patient-specific immunoglobulin (Ig) gene clonotype at diagnosis is a prerequisite for next-generation sequencing (NGS)-based measurable residual disease monitoring in multiple myeloma but may fail in low-tumor-burden samples. We evaluated the effect of CD138-positive plasma cell enrichment on Ig heavy chain ( IGH ) and Ig kappa ( IGK ) NGS clonotype detection in a paired split-sample cohort (N=20) and an unpaired cohort (N=60) tested before and after routine implementation of enrichment. In the paired cohort, enrichment numerically increased IGH detection from 65.0% to 85.0% and IGK detection from 65.0% to 80.0%. All 15 patients positive in both aliquots shared at least one exact sequence-matched clonotype. For dominant matched clonotypes, the clonal read fraction increased for IGH (median, 27.8% to 85.2%; P =0.0105) but not for IGK (33.6% to 36.9%; P =0.0942). Thus, CD138-positive plasma cell enrichment may increase NGS-based clonotype detection, although paired detection-rate differences were not statistically significant.
