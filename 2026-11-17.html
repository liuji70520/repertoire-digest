bioRxiv/2026-11-16/预印本 Preprint
做了什么What

对健康、腹泻与 SIV 感染猕猴的结肠、肠系膜淋巴结、血、脾做 TRG/TRD 组库测序，发现克隆结构呈明显组织分区、结肠富集 TRGV3；流式与高维聚类把循环与结肠 Vγ4⁺ 细胞定为 Vδ1⁺、效应记忆、T-bet 主导的终末分化谱系，受刺激后同时产 Th1/Th17 类细胞因子。

为什么重要Why

TRG/TRD repertoire sequencing across colon, mesenteric lymph node, blood and spleen in healthy, diarrheal and SIV-infected macaques showed compartment-specific clonal architecture with colonic enrichment of TRGV3; Vγ4+Vδ1+ cells were effector-memory, T-bet-dominant and dual Th1/Th17 cytokine producers.

Shows repertoire structure can define a gamma-delta subset's identity and tissue origin — a usable frame for our mucosal/liquid samples.

Turns clonal expansion from a correlate into a causal, regulatable step — exactly the missing link when we interpret expanded clones.

T-cell clonality and TCR repertoire dynamics during germinal center responses: A comprehensive review of regulatory mechanisms and therapeutic opportunities
International reviews of immunology · 2026-11-16 · 期刊 Journal · IF 2.9 · JCR Q3 · 中科院 4区
T-cell receptors (TCRs) are pivotal for antigen recognition within germinal centers (GCs), which are essential for B-cell maturation and high-affinity antibody production. Although the role played by T follicular helper (Tfh) cells in supporting B cells is established, the mechanisms driving the expansion and maintenance of distinct T-cell clonotypes in the GC perifollicular region remain poorly understood. This review aims to synthesize current knowledge on the differentiation, migration, and clonal expansion of T cells within GCs, with emphasis on Tfh-B-cell interactions. Findings from immunohistochemistry, flow cytometry, single-cell RNA sequencing, multiphoton microscopy, and CRISPR/Cas9-based approaches indicate that clonal expansion of T cells carrying defined TCR clonotypes is a regulated process driven by cognate Tfh-B-cell interactions. These interactions promote both B-cell affinity maturation and the concomitant selection and clonal expansion of T cells. The reviewed studies reveal that the TCR repertoire within the GC perifollicular region is refined over time, contributing to the development of long-lived memory and effector cells. The collective results underscore a dynamic reciprocity where T cells shape the antibody response while themselves undergoing antigen-driven selection. bioRxiv (immunology) · 2026-11-16 · 预印本 Preprint
V{gamma}4+ {gamma}{delta} T cells contribute to colonic homeostasis in humans, but their biology in nonhuman primates remains poorly defined. Here, we characterized V{gamma}4+ {gamma}{delta} T cells in blood and intestinal tissues of healthy, diarrhea-inflicted, and SIV-infected rhesus macaques. TRG/TRD repertoire sequencing across colon, mesenteric lymph node, blood, and spleen revealed compartment-specific clonal architecture and colonic enrichment of TRGV3. Flow cytometric profiling revealed circulating and colonic V{gamma}4+ T cells were predominantly V{delta}1+, exhibited an effector memory phenotype, and displayed T-bet-dominant transcriptional profiles. High-dimensional clustering confirmed V{gamma}4+V{delta}1+ T cells as a distinct terminally differentiated lineage. Functionally, stimulated V{gamma}4+ T cells exhibited robust Th1/Th17 cytokine production, supporting dual inflammatory and barrier-protective roles.
