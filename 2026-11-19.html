TIGIT⁺ CD8⁺ T 细胞：黑色素瘤外周血里可用的肿瘤反应性 TCR 富集池

A functional TIGIT-associated transcriptional program identifies circulating tumor-reactive CD8⁺ T cells in melanoma patients

Cancer Immunology Research/2026-11-17/期刊 JournalIF 8.2JCR Q1中科院 1区
做了什么What

把 TIGIT 作为肿瘤反应性 CD8⁺ T 细胞的功能性替代标志：血中 TIGIT⁺ CD8⁺ 细胞能杀伤自体肿瘤，且不依赖 PD-1 是否共表达；在克隆型层面，用 TIGIT 比用 PD-1 能捞到更宽的循环肿瘤反应性组库而保持高特异性；这群细胞与瘤内组库高度重叠，带效应记忆表型、DNA 低甲基化和克隆扩增；ICI 治疗的临床应答者血中 TIGIT⁺ CD8⁺ 频率升高；最后用配对的单细胞转录组+TCR 数据证明该转录程序能稳健识别循环肿瘤反应性克隆。

为什么重要Why

对我们最有用的是那条验证路径：不是靠标志物猜，而是用“自体肿瘤杀伤 + 与瘤内组库重叠 + 单细胞 TCR 配对”三重锚定血里的肿瘤反应性克隆。T-BCR 若要在外周血里找抗原特异/肿瘤反应性 T 细胞，这个“血克隆↔瘤内克隆”对应关系的建法和特异性口径可以直接借用；TIGIT 相对 PD-1 的“更宽而不更杂”也是个可复现的比较设计。

TIGIT marks blood CD8⁺ T cells that kill autologous tumor irrespective of PD-1; clonotype-level analysis shows TIGIT captures a broader circulating tumor-reactive repertoire than PD-1 at high specificity, closely mirroring the intratumoral repertoire, with effector-memory, DNA-hypomethylated and clonally expanded features; TIGIT⁺ CD8⁺ frequency rises in ICI responders; paired scRNA-seq plus TCR data confirm the program detects circulating tumor-reactive clones.

The validation chain — autologous killing, overlap with the intratumoral repertoire, and paired single-cell TCR — is a reusable template for identifying antigen-specific circulating clones from blood.

短暂抗 HIV-1 抗体治疗后的持久 CD8 缓解依赖 FcγR 参与——伴随早期克隆聚焦与克隆持久

Durable CD8-mediated remission after brief anti-HIV-1 antibody treatment requires FcγR engagement

PNAS/2026-11-17/期刊 JournalIF 9.1JCR Q1中科院 1区
做了什么What

在 SHIV 感染的猕猴里，用抗原结合、中和活性、药代都匹配、只差 FcγR 结合的 3BNC117 Fc 变体做对照：早期病毒控制相当，长期结局却截然不同——FcγR 结合型每只动物都出现持续近 300 周的 CD8 依赖抑制，FcγR 缺失型 8 只里只有 1 只。机制上，FcγR 介导的免疫调节“印刻”出一种 CD8 应答：抗原特异效应功能增强、早期克隆聚焦并维持克隆型持久、转录状态特征不同。

为什么重要Why

这是少见的把“抗体 Fc 工程 → T 细胞克隆行为 → 长期临床结局”三件事串起来的因果链，而且克隆聚焦/持久是做组库的人能直接量的指标。若我们要评估某种干预是否真的“训练”了 T 细胞免疫，这条“早期克隆聚焦 + 克隆持久”的组合可以作为组库侧的判读标准来用；也提示单纯看克隆多样性高低不够，要看聚焦得早不早、撑得久不久。

In SHIV-infected macaques, 3BNC117 Fc variants matched for antigen binding, neutralization and pharmacokinetics but differing in FcγR engagement gave comparable early virologic control yet divergent long-term outcomes: sustained CD8-dependent suppression for nearly 300 weeks in every FcγR-engaging recipient versus one of eight FcγR-null recipients; FcγR-driven immunomodulation imprinted enhanced effector function, early clonal focusing with sustained clonotype persistence, and distinct transcriptional states.

It causally links Fc engineering to T-cell clonal behaviour and durable outcome, and 'early clonal focusing plus sustained persistence' is a measurable repertoire readout we can adopt.
