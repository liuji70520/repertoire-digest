X-BCRdb：跨疾病 BCR 配对单细胞数据库，做克隆型—转录组联合分析

X-BCRdb: a cross-disease BCR-paired single-cell database for B-lineage-cell clonotype-transcriptome analysis

Nucleic Acids Research/2026-11-17/期刊 JournalIF 13.1JCR Q1中科院 2区
做了什么What

整合 61 项研究、1401 个样本，把单细胞 BCR 克隆型与 B 细胞转录状态、克隆演化放在同一个库里查询；作者指出既有免疫受体资源多偏 TCR、BCR 侧分析深度不足。

为什么重要Why

我们做的是 T 侧，但这份库的“克隆型↔转录状态↔跨疾病”数据模型正好可以照搬；将来 T-BCR 要建同类资源，字段设计与去重口径可直接参考。

Harmonizes 61 studies and 1,401 samples into a queryable resource linking single-cell BCR clonotypes to B-cell transcriptional states and clonal evolution, motivated by TCR-biased existing resources.

Its clonotype-to-transcriptome data model is directly reusable if we build a T-side resource.

异体 CMV 特异 T 细胞联合帕博利珠单抗治疗复发胶质母细胞瘤的 I 期试验

Phase I Clinical Trial of Allogeneic Cytomegalovirus-specific T Cells in Combination with Pembrolizumab for Recurrent Glioblastoma

medRxiv/2026-11-17/预印本 Preprint
做了什么What

3+3 剂量爬坡的 I 期研究：复发 GBM 患者接受 4 次每周异体 CMV 特异 T 细胞（CYT-101）输注，随后联合帕博利珠单抗，主要终点为安全性与免疫学活性。

为什么重要Why

“抗原特异 T 细胞作为活药 + 检查点阻断”的临床范式；若我们后续想用组库追踪回输细胞的扩增与持久性，这类试验的监测设计（何时采血、看哪些克隆）是现成模板。

A 3+3 dose-escalation phase I study giving four weekly infusions of allogeneic CMV-specific T cells (CYT-101) followed by pembrolizumab in recurrent GBM, with safety and immunological activity as endpoints.

A clinical template for antigen-specific T cells as living drugs, including how to monitor their expansion and persistence by repertoire profiling.

https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.11.17.26367100v1

X-BCRdb: a cross-disease BCR-paired single-cell database for B-lineage-cell clonotype-transcriptome analysis
Nucleic acids research · 2026-11-17 · 期刊 Journal · IF 13.1 · JCR Q1 · 中科院 2区
B cells and their clonally rearranged B-cell receptors (BCRs) form the cellular and molecular basis of humoral immunity. Single-cell RNA sequencing coupled with V(D)J profiling now enables BCR clonotypes to be linked directly to B-cell transcriptional states; however, existing immune-receptor resources are often T-cell/TCR-oriented or limited in BCR-specific analytical depth. Here, we present X-BCRdb, a cross-disease single-cell database for integrated analysis of BCR repertoires, B-cell transcriptional states, and clonal evolution. The current release harmonizes 61 studies, 1401 samples, and 903 subjects across 45 disease or physiological categories and 13 tissue groups. It contains 2 402 995 processed B-lineage cells, including 1 565 870 cells with matched transcriptome and BCR information, and 3 993 244 annotated BCR records. A unified analytical workflow supports standardized B-cell annotation and BCR repertoire characterization, including clonotype assignment, SHM profiling, isotype analysis, VH/VL pairing, lineage reconstruction, and public clonotype analysis. The web interface links these receptor features with B-cell transcriptional states across individual samples, studies and diseases, and provides global CDR3 similarity search, data download, and API access. Phase I Clinical Trial of Allogeneic Cytomegalovirus-specific T Cells in Combination with Pembrolizumab for Recurrent Glioblastoma
medRxiv · 2026-11-17 · 预印本 Preprint
Purpose: Glioblastoma (GBM) remains refractory to immunotherapy due to its immunosuppressive microenvironment. Tumour-infiltrating cytomegalovirus (CMV)-specific T cells express elevated programmed cell death protein-1 (PD-1), suggesting checkpoint-mediated dysfunction may limit T-cell therapy. We evaluated the safety and immunological activity of allogeneic CMV-specific T-cell therapy (CYT-101) combined with pembrolizumab. Patients and Methods: In this phase I 3+3 dose-escalation study, recurrent GBM patients received four weekly infusions of CYT-101 followed by pembrolizumab. The primary endpoint was safety. Secondary endpoints included progression-free survival at 6 months (PFS-6), overall survival at 6 months (OS-6), and immune correlates of response. Results: CYT-101 T-cell therapy demonstrated polyfunctional activity, broad HLA restriction, potent cytolytic function, and diverse T-cell receptor repertoires. Nine patients received study treatment. No dose-limiting toxicities were observed, and the maximum tolerated dose was not reached. Only six adverse events were considered treatment-related, and no treatment-related serious adverse events, graft-versus-host disease, cytokine release syndrome, or neurotoxicity occurred.
