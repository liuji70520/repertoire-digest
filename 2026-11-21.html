TRIOPS：不依赖肽段的深度学习从 TCR 序列预测 HLA 限制性，并在肿瘤中捕捉偏位等位基因的 HLA 丢失

Peptide-independent deep learning with TRIOPS predicts TCR–HLA restriction and recovers locus-biased HLA loss in tumors

Research Square/2026-11-19/预印本 Preprint
做了什么What

用双分支卷积模型、交叉反应感知采样训练，仅凭氨基酸序列预测 TCR–HLA 限制性：留出 TCRαβ 对 AUC 0.97、训练中未见的 HLA 等位基因 0.92、未见过的 TCR 0.81。再把 8,906 例 TCGA 肿瘤（23 种癌型）的克隆型指派到最可能的限制性等位基因，发现在 HLA-A/B 杂合对里 engagement 系统性偏向低表达的那个等位基因（一致率 0.60 / 0.65，23/23 癌型）。

为什么重要Why

克隆型表目前只能回答“扩增了多少”，回答不了“认什么”。这条把 HLA 限制性变成可从序列推断的属性，等于给组库加了第二维注释；在肿瘤里还能反过来当 HLA 丢失的读数，正好补上我们做 TCR 克隆分析缺的那一半。

A dual-branch convolutional model trained with cross-reactivity-aware sampling predicts TCR–HLA restriction from amino acid sequence alone (AUC 0.97 on held-out TCRαβ pairs, 0.92 on HLA alleles withheld from training, 0.81 on unseen TCRs), then assigns clonotypes from 8,906 TCGA tumours across 23 cancer types to their likeliest restricting allele; engagement is systematically biased to the lower-expressed allele of HLA-A/B heterozygotes (concordance 0.60 and 0.65, 23/23 cancers).

Clonotype tables answer how much a clone expanded, never what it recognises; this makes HLA restriction a sequence-inferable property, adding a second annotation axis and doubling as an HLA-loss readout in tumours.

长读长 nanopore 测序：CLL 的 IGHV 基因分析又快又准

Long-read nanopore sequencing: A rapid and highly accurate approach for IGHV gene analysis in chronic lymphocytic leukaemia

British journal of haematology/2026-11-19/期刊 JournalIF 3.8JCR Q1中科院 2区
做了什么What

60 例 CLL 外周血，EuroClonality 引物一步 PCR 加同步 barcode，12 个样本一个 run，MinION 上每样本过滤后仍有 20 万条以上 Q30 读长；直接读全长 IGHV-IGHD-IGHJ 重排，所有病例都检出优势克隆型（平均丰度 75%），基因使用与体细胞高频突变状态与 Illumina 结果高度一致，占比 >5% 的次要重排也能稳定识别。

为什么重要Why

全长重排和 SHM 定量本来要拆成两步，长读长一次做完，省掉片段拼接与位点推断；这套多重 barcode + 单步 PCR 的工程流程，是我们把配对组库搬到液基/微量样本上能直接借的模板（注意这里测的是 BCR，只借方法）。

Peripheral blood from 60 CLL patients was amplified in a single PCR step with EuroClonality-based primers plus simultaneous barcoding, multiplexing 12 samples per MinION run and yielding over 200,000 Q30 reads per sample; full-length IGHV-IGHD-IGHJ rearrangements gave a dominant productive clonotype in every case (mean abundance 75%), with gene usage and SHM status highly concordant with Illumina and stable detection of secondary rearrangements above 5%.

Long reads fold full-length rearrangement and SHM quantification into one pass; the multiplexed single-step barcoding workflow is a directly reusable template for paired repertoire work on liquid-based and low-input samples (BCR here — borrow the method only).

Peptide-independent deep learning with TRIOPS predicts TCR–HLA restriction and recovers locus-biased HLA loss in tumors
Research Square · 2026-11-19 · 预印本 Preprint
Abstract T cell receptor (TCR) antigen recognition is MHC-restricted, yet the restricting HLA allele is unknown for nearly all sequenced receptors. We present TRIOPS, a dual-branch convolutional model that predicts TCR–HLA restriction from amino acid sequence alone, trained with cross-reactivity-aware sampling. TRIOPS reaches an AUC of 0.97 on held-out TCRαβ pairs, 0.92 on HLA alleles withheld from training, and 0.81 on unseen TCRs. We tested whether predicted engagement tracks allele-specific HLA loss in 8,906 TCGA tumors across 23 cancer types, assigning each clonotype to its likeliest restricting allele within the corresponding tumor genotype. At HLA-A and HLA-B, engagement was higher at the lower-expressed allele of each heterozygous pair (concordance 0.60 and 0.65; 23/23 cancers). TRIOPS resolves this locus-dependent pattern from receptor sequences and HLA genotypes alone, establishing HLA-restricted TCR engagement as a relevant measurable feature of the T cell receptor repertoire.
