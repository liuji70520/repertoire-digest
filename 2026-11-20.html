TIA1 与 TIAL1 用转录后调控把 T 细胞摁在静息态，维持 naive 组库的底盘

Posttranscriptional regulation by TIA1 and TIAL1 controls the transcriptional program enforcing T cell quiescence

Science Advances/2026-11-18/期刊 JournalIF 12.5JCR Q1中科院 1区
做了什么What

把两个 RNA 结合蛋白 TIA1 与 TIAL1 认定为 CD4/CD8 T 细胞静息的促进因子：双缺失的 T 细胞在没有同源抗原的情况下就提前活化、耗竭、死亡；机制上它们促进 Foxp1、LEF1、TCF1 这些静息相关转录因子的 mRNA 翻译，从而压住稳态增殖与活化。

为什么重要Why

我们平时看的是“组库有多宽”，这篇看的是“宽度靠什么维持”——naive 细胞一旦提前活化耗竭，组库多样性就在没有抗原刺激的情况下自己塌掉。如果后续要解释某个样本 naive compartment 偏小、或克隆型多样性偏低，这是一条可查的上游机制（转录后层面，不是克隆选择层面）；也提示 naive 池的“静”是主动过程，做轨迹/拟时序时别把它当成默认的静息终点。

Identifies the RNA-binding proteins TIA1 and TIAL1 as promoters of CD4 and CD8 T cell quiescence: double-deficient T cells become prematurely activated, exhausted and die without cognate antigen, because these proteins drive mRNA translation of quiescence-associated transcription factors Foxp1, LEF1 and TCF1 that restrain homeostatic proliferation and activation.

We usually ask how broad a repertoire is; this asks what keeps it broad. Loss of naive-cell quiescence collapses diversity without antigenic pressure, offering an upstream, posttranscriptional explanation to check when a naive compartment or clonal diversity looks unexpectedly small.

https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeh4461

Posttranscriptional regulation by TIA1 and TIAL1 controls the transcriptional program enforcing T cell quiescence
Science advances · 2026-11-18 · 期刊 Journal · IF 12.5 · JCR Q1 · 中科院 1区
Immune protection against infections relies on long-term maintenance of a highly diversified naïve T cell repertoire. Transcription factors cooperate to enforce T cell metabolic quiescence and maintenance. However, less is known about the posttranscriptional networks that preserve naïve T cells. Here, we describe the RNA binding proteins T cell antigen 1 (TIA1) and TIA-like 1 (TIAL1) as key promoters of CD4 and CD8 T cell quiescence. T cells deficient in TIA1 and TIAL1 undergo premature T cell activation, exhaustion, and death in the absence of cognate antigens. Mechanistically, TIA1 and TIAL1 promote the messenger RNA translation of quiescence-associated transcription factors such as forkhead box P1, lymphoid enhancer factor 1, and T cell factor 1 to restrain homeostatic T cell proliferation and activation. In summary, our study highlights a previously unrecognized dependency on posttranscriptional gene regulation by TIA1 and TIAL1 for implementing the quiescent transcriptional programs for long survival of T cells.
