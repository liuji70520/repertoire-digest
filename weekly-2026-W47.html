短暂抗 HIV-1 抗体治疗后的持久 CD8 缓解依赖 FcγR 参与——伴随早期克隆聚焦与克隆持久

Durable CD8-mediated remission after brief anti-HIV-1 antibody treatment requires FcγR engagement

PNAS/2026-11-17/期刊 JournalIF 9.1JCR Q1中科院 1区
做了什么What

在 SHIV 感染的猕猴里，用抗原结合、中和活性、药代都匹配、只差 FcγR 结合的 3BNC117 Fc 变体做对照：早期病毒控制相当，长期结局却截然不同——FcγR 结合型每只动物都出现持续近 300 周的 CD8 依赖抑制，FcγR 缺失型 8 只里只有 1 只。机制上，FcγR 介导的免疫调节“印刻”出一种 CD8 应答：抗原特异效应功能增强、早期克隆聚焦并维持克隆型持久、转录状态特征不同。

为什么重要Why

这是少见的把“抗体 Fc 工程 → T 细胞克隆行为 → 长期临床结局”三件事串起来的因果链，而且克隆聚焦/持久是做组库的人能直接量的指标。若我们要评估某种干预是否真的“训练”了 T 细胞免疫，这条“早期克隆聚焦 + 克隆持久”的组合可以作为组库侧的判读标准来用；也提示单纯看克隆多样性高低不够，要看聚焦得早不早、撑得久不久。

In SHIV-infected macaques, 3BNC117 Fc variants matched for antigen binding, neutralization and pharmacokinetics but differing in FcγR engagement gave comparable early virologic control yet divergent long-term outcomes: sustained CD8-dependent suppression for nearly 300 weeks in every FcγR-engaging recipient versus one of eight FcγR-null recipients; FcγR-driven immunomodulation imprinted enhanced effector function, early clonal focusing with sustained clonotype persistence, and distinct transcriptional states.

It causally links Fc engineering to T-cell clonal behaviour and durable outcome, and 'early clonal focusing plus sustained persistence' is a measurable repertoire readout we can adopt.

Science advances · 2026-11-18 · 期刊 Journal · IF 12.5 · JCR Q1 · 中科院 1区
Immune protection against infections relies on long-term maintenance of a highly diversified naïve T cell repertoire. Transcription factors cooperate to enforce T cell metabolic quiescence and maintenance. However, less is known about the posttranscriptional networks that preserve naïve T cells. Here, we describe the RNA binding proteins T cell antigen 1 (TIA1) and TIA-like 1 (TIAL1) as key promoters of CD4 and CD8 T cell quiescence. T cells deficient in TIA1 and TIAL1 undergo premature T cell activation, exhaustion, and death in the absence of cognate antigens.
